The Way You Hold Others

Five chambers about how you show up for the people you care about. Not what you intend — what you actually do when someone needs you.

What this crossing offers: language for how you love, and what that costs and gives you.

Five chambers. Five questions drawn from emotional intelligence research. The Veil shows you which of four archetypes you carry most strongly. Not a diagnosis — a reflection. Choose honestly, not strategically.
